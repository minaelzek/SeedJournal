# Seed Journal — Living Sakura v2

## Product promise

The Sakura is the emotional engine of Seed Journal. It is a persistent living
companion, not a score display. Every intentionally completed journal creates
one equally meaningful memory blossom and at least one additional visible
development. Private reflective depth may enrich the surrounding development,
but it never determines whether a memory deserves beauty.

The product loop is:

```text
write → reflect → nurture → witness exactly what changed → feel attachment → return
```

The tree never decays, shames, compares, or punishes absence.

## Character and silhouette

The mature character is grounded, resilient, open, and gently leaning into the
world. The user should eventually recognize their tree from its silhouette.

- One aged trunk leaning 3–6° before separating into two unequal leaders.
- Five to seven primary boughs, including one signature low sweeping branch.
- Fourteen to twenty-two secondary branches and fifty to ninety tertiaries.
- Three front-to-back crown layers and at least three protected canopy windows.
- Crown width-to-height ratio 1.10–1.25 with a 3–7% displaced centroid.
- Left/right visual-mass difference 10–18%.
- Peak bloom retains 25–35% negative space and 35–45% visible wood.
- Ten to eighteen percent of terminal gestures descend slightly under gravity.
- Parent radius follows the pipe model within roughly 15% at major forks.

The leafless silhouette must be beautiful and unmistakably Sakura before any
blossom system may hide it.

## Botanical language

- Roots widen naturally from the trunk, then disappear into the ground. No
  radial fins or pointed feet.
- Branches attach parent-first through visible collars and continuous taper.
- The trunk uses cool taupe, ash brown, and grey umber planes with horizontal
  lenticel accents and restrained warm highlights.
- Flowering clusters contain 3–7 open flowers, 1–4 buds, occasional half-open
  flowers, and visible short pedicels.
- Open flowers have five distinguishable, slightly notched and cupped petals.
- Peak-bloom sites are only 50–65% occupied; flowers form passages, not foam.
- Spring leaves are pointed copper/olive/ochre accents amounting to 8–15% of
  visible floral elements.

## Living motion

Motion is hierarchical and irregular, never a whole-tree pulse.

| Layer | Idle range |
|---|---:|
| Trunk | 0.15–0.35° |
| Primary boughs | 0.3–0.8° |
| Secondary branches | 0.8–1.8° |
| Fine twigs | 1.5–3.5° |
| Leaves | 2–6° |
| Blossoms | 1–4° |

Base wind cycles last 8–14 seconds. Irregular gentle gusts arrive roughly every
25–45 seconds. Child motion inherits its parent and adds a delayed local
response. Loose petals are rare and localized.

Pointer, touch, keyboard focus, and memory selection apply contained attention
to one branch neighborhood. Memory copy remains in stable interface space.

Reduced-motion mode keeps the same final state and interaction semantics while
using light drift and short dissolves instead of sway, camera travel, or petals.

## Private development grammar

Internal tiers are never shown, announced, logged, or sent to the server in
plaintext. Every tier begins with the same meaningful memory blossom.

| Private input | Surrounding development |
|---|---|
| 1 | Leaf, bud, flowering spur, or short terminal extension |
| 2 | Short twig with 2–4 accompanying organs |
| 4 | Branchlet or paired twigs with 5–8 accompanying organs |
| 6 | Architectural development, branch thickening, 8–14 organs, and a restrained bloom/light event |

Recipe variants are deterministic and numerous enough that leaf/bud/blossom
does not become a visible grade ladder. Repetition, word count, punctuation,
and emotion vocabulary cannot independently increase richness. Uncertainty is
resolved generously. A user may decline every optional prompt and still receive
a complete, beautiful reward.

Every memory receives a permanent identifier, stable botanical address, parent
branch relationship, and revisitable location. Revisits grow beside the
original memory. Luminous treatment denotes user-marked importance, continuing
revisit lineage, or a life chapter—not writing quality.

## Continuing development

Maturity is a life chapter, not a terminal state. The event ledger and nurture
index are unbounded. Rendering remains bounded by:

- an append-stable logical botanical graph;
- 192–256 explicit structural segments at a time;
- 12–20 stable canopy clusters;
- approximately 2,500 visible organ instances;
- 24–48 active hit regions plus an accessible full-history navigator;
- deterministic compaction of distant terminal detail without relocating or
  deleting historical memory addresses.

After maturity, structural expansion slows but never reaches zero. Development
continues through terminal shoots, branch thickening, bark history, root
character, blossom composition, canopy pockets, seasonal expression, and
memory neighborhoods.

## Growth reveal

The same persistent tree used by the garden performs the reveal.

1. Commit encrypted state (0–300 ms).
2. Find the exact growth locus (300–800 ms).
3. Quiet the wind and pulse through the parent branch (800–1,200 ms).
4. Extend new wood with continuous attachment (1,200–2,800 ms).
5. Open the memory blossom and accompanying organs (2,000–3,400 ms).
6. Settle the light and release at most one or two petals (3,400–4,200 ms).

Reveal duration stays broadly consistent across private tiers so ceremony does
not expose an assessment.

## Cutover gates

The product currently ships Living Sakura as the garden renderer, but **founder
visual approval of the still-pack is still required** before treating cutover as
closed. Capture tooling and checklist:

→ [`living-sakura-still-pack.md`](./living-sakura-still-pack.md)  
→ `.living-sakura/renders/` (generated PNGs)

Gates the founder must explicitly approve:

1. Leafless mature silhouette from front, three-quarter, side, and rear.
2. Peak-bloom mature tree from the same views.
3. Seed, sprout, sapling, young, established, mature, and enduring chapters.
4. A 60-second idle-life capture without an obvious loop.
5. Pointer, touch, keyboard, and memory attention.
6. One simple and one rich growth reveal from the same starting tree.
7. The reveal inside the real journal completion flow.
8. Stable memories and observable development at entries 1, 193, 1,000, and 10,000.
9. Desktop/mobile/reduced-motion performance and accessibility evidence.

No agent confidence score can substitute for this approval.
